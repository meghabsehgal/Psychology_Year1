Piaget vs Kohlberg: Cognitive & Moral Development

Piaget — Cognitive

  • Concrete operations stage
  • Decentration (multiple dimensions)
  • Conservation (number → volume)
  • Seriation and class inclusion
  • Inductive reasoning develops

Piaget — Moral

  • Heteronomous morality (6–7)
  • Rules = fixed & absolute
  • Punishment is inevitable
  • Autonomous morality (9–10)
  • Intentions matter, rules flexible

Kohlberg's Levels

  • Level 1: Punishment avoidance
  • Level 1: Satisfying own needs
  • Level 2: Good-boy/nice-girl
  • Level 2: Law and order
  • Level 3: Social contract & ethics

Dewey vs Russell

  • Dewey: child-centred, instincts
  • Dewey: activity-based learning
  • Russell: literature for writing
  • Russell: accuracy in arithmetic
  • Both: curiosity is central

LD vs MR vs ADHD vs Gifted: Quick Comparison

Learning Disability

  • Normal or above IQ
  • Specific skill deficits only
  • Neurological origin
  • Dyslexia / Dysgraphia / Dyscalculia
  • Direct & multisensory instruction

Mental Retardation

  • IQ below 70
  • Adaptive behaviour deficits
  • Genetic or environmental causes
  • Mild → Profound severity
  • Functional curriculum, inclusion

ADHD

  • Normal IQ possible
  • Inattentive or Hyperactive type
  • Neurological / dietary causes
  • Distractibility, impulsiveness
  • Medication + behaviour therapy

Gifted

  • High IQ or specific talent
  • May have social difficulties
  • Bored with regular curriculum
  • Needs enrichment activities
  • Advanced & independent study
