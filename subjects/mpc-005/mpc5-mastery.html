Structural Framework

### Key Technical & Educational Highlights of this Application: 1. **Interactive Active-Recall Architecture:** All sections (Exam Essentials, Retention Hooks, and Field Applications) are hidden by default behind custom collapsible cards. This design encourages you to actively test your memory before reviewing the core IGNOU parameters. 2. **Comprehensive Syllabus Coverage:** Features 12 full modules covering all four Blocks of the IGNOU MPC-005 syllabus, including detailed scientific frameworks, true vs. quasi-experimental designs, variable classifications, and qualitative methods. 3. **High-Yield Content Standard:** Built specifically to help you target 10-mark and 6-mark answers on the exam. It includes technical terms, researcher names (like McGuigan, Kerlinger, Glaser & Strauss, Yin, and Campbell & Stanley), and statistical concepts (such as variance types, measurement scales, and internal validity threats). 4. **Non-Verbal Memory Hooks:** Replaces standard text mnemonics with clear physical analogies—like *Industrial Water Filtration Plants*, *Earthquake-Proof Blueprints*, and *Forensic Crash Site Investigations*—to make abstract research concepts easier to understand and remember. 5. **Interactive Chart.js Modeling:** Includes embedded visual components to help you analyze complex structures, such as error-margin reductions, variance allocations, and sampling-design distributions.
